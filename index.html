Built with AI, end to end

Directed by Andrius Bubliauskas. Claude Code by Anthropic writes, plans and reviews the code.

Tested to the last rule

2,005 automated tests check the game's rules on the main line, and two different compilers give identical results.

A different kind of space combat

Six degrees of freedom: you move and turn freely along every axis. One heavy ship, its nose aimed with the mouse. Ships are machines, breaking along their seams.

Where it is going

Built so far: the rules core, the demo's rules, ships as machines, game modes, balance and sub-system combat, air, orbit and descent, and the engine-free part of first flight (cameras, controls, menus and a replay tool). M4a, the engine-neutral half of M4, was built on its own branch by 10 November, ahead of its merge into the main line. Next: M3b, the first flight in the game engine, Unreal Engine 5.8.4, once the engine is installed; then M4b, the engine half of M4, and with it the clean v0 demo, the first playable version.

Estimate of 31 October 2026: about 67 billion tokens of AI work to go, about 11 weeks at the current pace.
