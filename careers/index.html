Internships at Orion-81

Orion-81 is a space combat game built by a small team that directs AI. Every internship here works with Claude, and with Unreal Engine 5 or Blender where the role calls for it. You contribute to a real project and grow your portfolio.

  • 3D Asset Artist, AI-assisted (intern)
  • Level Designer, AI-assisted (intern)
  • Backend Engineer (intern)
  • Webmaster (intern)
  • PR Manager (intern)
  • Full-Stack Developer (intern)

3D Asset Artist, AI-assisted (intern)

Internship · Unpaid · Remote · 3 months · Hours of your choosing

About the role

You make the game's ships and props in Blender, working with Claude to turn a written specification into a model.

What you will do

  • Build hulls and props in Blender with Claude.
  • Keep models clean and to scale, ready to export as GLB.
  • Bring them into Unreal Engine 5 and check how they look.
  • Keep each asset's source and notes in the repository.

How it works

The first month is a probation period. At its start you are given one clear deliverable for the end of the month. How many hours you put in is up to you: you pass probation by delivering it, or you do not.

Requirements

Education: high school completed, as a minimum.

Nice to have

Experience with GitHub or GitLab, with AI tools, with Blender, or with Unreal Engine.

What you get

A free seat in Orion-81's Claude organisation, which you may also use for your own learning. The chance to contribute to the project and to grow your portfolio.
